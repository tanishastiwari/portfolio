How I work

Four moves I repeat on every campaign, whatever the channel.

  1. Step 1

    Understand the audience and commercial objective

    Who are we talking to, and what should happen next: awareness, registrations, MQLs or pipeline? For ABM, it starts with the account list.

  2. Step 2

    Build the story, content and campaign plan

    One clear message, then the assets that carry it: case studies, articles, landing pages, email sequences and event collateral, mapped onto a content calendar.

  3. Step 3

    Activate across relevant channels with sales, product and creative teams

    LinkedIn, email, SEO and paid social, plus Spotify, YouTube and Demandbase where the audience is. Business development representatives (BDRs) and sales stay in the loop, so follow-up is ready when the leads arrive.

  4. Step 4

    Measure engagement, lead quality and pipeline progression

    MQLs, sales-qualified leads (SQLs) and opportunities tracked through the customer relationship management (CRM) system, reported in Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Looker Studio dashboards, and fed into the next campaign.
