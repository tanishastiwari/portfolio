How I work

The practical sequence behind most of my campaigns, from the first brief to the pipeline review.

  1. 01

    Understand the audience and commercial objective

    I start with who we need to reach and what the business needs from them: the target accounts or segment, and whether the goal is awareness, registrations, MQLs or pipeline.

  2. 02

    Build the story, content and campaign plan

    I shape the message into the formats the plan needs, such as case studies, articles, landing pages, email sequences and event collateral, and set the content calendar that keeps it on track.

  3. 03

    Activate across relevant channels with sales, product and creative teams

    LinkedIn, email, SEO, paid social, Spotify, YouTube or Demandbase, depending on the audience. I work alongside sales, product, creative and business development teams so the campaign and the follow-up move together.

  4. 04

    Measure engagement, lead quality and pipeline progression

    I track MQLs, sales-qualified leads (SQLs) and opportunities, keep customer relationship management (CRM) attribution clean, and build Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Looker Studio dashboards so the next campaign starts from evidence.
